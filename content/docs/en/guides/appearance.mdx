## Themes

Open **Settings → Appearance** to choose a preset. The client includes more than thirty themes, including Catppuccin, Dracula, Gruvbox, Tokyo Night, Rosé Pine, Everforest, and Solarized.

The theme editor lets you build your own palette, choose a font, and add a background image with blur and other effects. Plugins can also contribute [themes and fonts](/en/docs/plugins/content).

## System theme

The **System** theme follows OS light and dark mode. Wallpaper colours are read from the primary display on macOS and Windows, and from supported Linux desktops: GNOME, KDE Plasma, Xfce, Cinnamon, and MATE.

Colours refresh on focus and every 30 seconds while System is selected. GNOME timed XML wallpapers use the first still image. When a wallpaper cannot be read, the app uses its default light or dark colours. A manually chosen accent takes priority.

On Android 12 and newer, System uses Material You wallpaper colours.

## Languages

**Settings → Language** offers English, Russian, Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese, and Persian, or can follow the system language. Changes apply immediately.

Persian uses a right-to-left layout. Logs stay in English so that diagnostics remain consistent across languages.

## Keep your customisation

[Backups](/en/docs/guides/backups) include themes, fonts, and background pictures alongside your connection and routing settings.
