---
title: "Routing setups and schedules"
description: "Save separate routes, import .vflow files, and switch setups by time."
---

A setup contains a complete routing configuration. Keep separate setups for home, work, or another use, and switch from **Routing** or the desktop tray.

## Create and switch

Use the setup controls in **Routing** to create, duplicate, rename, or delete a setup. Pick its name to make it active. Deleting a setup removes all its rules and cannot be undone.

On desktop, you can list or switch setups from the [command line](/en/docs/reference/cli):

```bash
NuggetVPN setup
NuggetVPN setup Work
```

## Start from a preset

Built-in presets include blocking ads and trackers, blocking QUIC, sending torrents directly, and bypassing the VPN for a country.

Review the added rules and their order. In particular, **Torrents direct** and **Direct for a country** intentionally send matching traffic outside the VPN.

## Geo data and remote lists

Service and country rules can use custom GeoSite and GeoIP data. Manage the files through the routing geo-data controls. Category suggestions depend on the data you have loaded.

Remote rule lists may be downloaded when you connect and refreshed by the core. The editor shows their status and provides **Update lists now**. Make sure the chosen core supports the format: mihomo skips sing-box `.srs` and `.json` lists and uses its own geo data.

## Share a .vflow file

Use **Routing → Export** to save a routing setup and **Import** to load one. A `.vflow` file contains routing, not a complete app backup.

Specific-server destinations depend on profile IDs on the original computer. Those references may be missing on another installation; review them after importing. Generic VPN, Direct, and Block destinations are easier to share.

Plugins can supply setups under **Routing → From plugins**. Applying one replaces the current routing, and you can undo that action.

## Schedule a setup

The schedule controls in **Routing** can switch setups by time of the week. Choose the setup, weekdays, and start and end times—for example, **Work** on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00.

Review the schedule after changing the computer’s time or timezone. The app must be running for its scheduling controls to take effect.
